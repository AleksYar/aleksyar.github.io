How to read the results

  • TSP20 means a travelling-salesperson problem with 20 cities.
  • A complete or valid tour visits every city exactly once and returns to the start. An optimal tour has the shortest reference length.
  • Optimal counts use each report’s stated evaluation denominator. Gaps are calculated only over valid tours; no valid tours means the gap is undefined.
  • Fine-tuning headlines use final validation results. Prompts, datasets, decoding and scoring differ between experiment families; paired comparisons identify what can be compared.
  • The learned TSP head and structured decoder are specific to routing. General-language checks use a different decoding mode.
