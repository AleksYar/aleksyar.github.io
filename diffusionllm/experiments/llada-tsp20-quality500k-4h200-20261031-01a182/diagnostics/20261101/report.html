500k token-quality experiment: failure diagnosis

The run completed, but generation usually stretched a short tour into hundreds of repeated city tokens. The strongest observed mismatch is the treatment of answer boundaries and padding during training.

All 500,000 stored records were scanned. Response lengths including EOT: {87: 500000}. Invalid clean or shuffled permutations: 0.

  1. Quality training excludes all padding from attention and both losses. The earlier QKV20k objective includes EOS padding as response targets.
  2. Training responses end at EOT at the last valid position; evaluation supplies 512 unpadded output positions. All-masked inference therefore differs substantially in answer length and boundary context.
  3. 87/100 answers in each 500k condition place their first stop token at position 511; the QKV20k control emits 86 visible tokens in every case.
  4. Synthetic corruptions preserve a 21-field permutation. They do not provide negative examples of duplicate cities, long routes, malformed formatting, or repeated padding-region text.
  5. The last 1000 training updates detect 94% of visible synthetic bad tokens, but inference remasks a median of only 3 tokens per answer.

Evidence limit: The padding/boundary mismatch is the strongest concrete explanation for length inflation. Synthetic-corruption distribution mismatch plausibly explains weak remasking of repeated outputs. These are supported hypotheses, not a controlled causal attribution. No additional GPU experiment was run.
