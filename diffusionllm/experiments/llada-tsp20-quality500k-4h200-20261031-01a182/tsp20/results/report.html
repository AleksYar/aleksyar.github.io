LLADA-8B-INSTRUCT · H200 → FOUR L40S CONTINUATION · FRESH QKV ADAPTER + TOKEN-QUALITY HEAD

TSP20 LoRA experiment

Status: complete · phase complete · update 31250/31250

500,000 training examples; 100 fixed validation cases. One epoch, LoRA rank 64, alpha 64, learning rate 0.0001. Fresh stock LLaDA weights and fresh optimizer; Q/K/V adapters and a 16,793,601-parameter token-quality head (67,125,249 total trainable parameters). Resumed microbatch 1 per GPU, accumulation 4, effective batch 16; 31,250 updates. Two training views per example; visible-token BCE plus 0.5 masked reconstruction CE. With probability 0.6, derange 2–6 interior city slots; random response masks are independent in the two views. Final evaluation uses 512 positions, 128 diffusion steps, temperature 0, seed 42, one attempt, no repairs. After-stock uses the trained vocabulary with the head bypassed; after uses quality-guided remasking with the same 128-call budget. Before is the reused stock-model baseline on these exact 100 cases; its original provenance is preserved in baseline-reuse.json (local file). Baseline generation time belongs to the original run.

Complete tours count every city exactly once and close the cycle. Gap is computed only for complete tours, using coordinate distances. Invalid answers have no gap.
