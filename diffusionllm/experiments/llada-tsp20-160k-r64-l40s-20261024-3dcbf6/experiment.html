12.5 GPU-hours of training; plan 15–18 hours including validation and overhead. The midpoint is reached after about 6.2 hours of training, plus the initial validation and any interruptions. CPU data preparation and queue time are additional.

Each permanent checkpoint includes the adapter, optimizer, scheduler, training order and random state. Two recent recovery checkpoints are also retained. Every validation answer and its score is saved.

Training and data

Fresh rank-64 adapter with alpha 64 and dropout 0.05. BF16 frozen base; FP32 adapter. Microbatch 2 and eight accumulation passes; effective batch 16. One epoch, AdamW, learning rate 0.0001, cosine schedule and 500 warmup updates. Activation checkpointing stays off.

100k existing verified training cases plus 60k new unique TSP20 cases, generated and exactly labelled using eight workers on your Mac CPU. All 160k are shuffled together. The fixed validation set is excluded from training. Validation uses one answer per case, 512 output positions and 128 diffusion steps, with no route repair.

Your Mac prepares and checks the dataset first. The GPU job is submitted only after the verified data has been uploaded to Newton. A short integration check verifies the actual runner and adapter save/reload before training starts fresh.
