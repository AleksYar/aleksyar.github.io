What changed

The extra 80k examples increased optimal tours from 19 to 36 and lowered the mean valid gap from 3.08% to 1.75%. On the 83 cases valid at both stages, 44 tours improved, 20 tied, and 19 worsened; 8 previously invalid answers became valid while 6 became invalid.

Compared with the previous 100k rank-16 run, optimal tours rose from 20 to 36 and mean valid gap fell from 2.35% to 1.75% (25.7% lower). Valid-tour counts changed from 90 to 91. This comparison changes data volume, rank, adapter scaling and learning-rate schedule, so it does not isolate the effect of rank.

The CPU nearest-neighbor + 2-opt baseline remains stronger overall: all 100 tours valid, 43 optimal, and a 1.35% mean gap. These are validation results, not an untouched final test.

The model’s printed objective values remain inaccurate. None of its 91 valid final tours had a stated length matching the recomputed length at the required three-decimal precision. All reported gaps use distances recalculated from coordinates.

Actual compute

Training updates took 12.60 hours, validation generation 1.73 hours, and model loading, profile checks, checkpoint saving, reporting and other overhead about 19.7 minutes. The total was slightly below the planned 15–18 hours.

All 10,000 updates completed with microbatch 2, accumulation 8 and activation checkpointing disabled. Exactly 160,000 distinct training examples were used once. Average update time was 4.536 seconds; no memory fallback was recorded. The 500k one-epoch training-only projection at this observed speed is 39.4 L40S hours, before validation and other overhead.

Data preparation ran on the Mac CPU in 3m49s before GPU allocation. Job 1460664 completed with exit code 0:0 on nlp-l40-1.
