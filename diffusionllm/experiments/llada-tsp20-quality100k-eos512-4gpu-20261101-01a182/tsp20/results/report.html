TSP20 LoRA experiment

Status: complete · phase complete · update 6250/6250

100,000 training examples; 100 fixed validation cases. One epoch, LoRA rank 64, alpha 64, learning rate 0.0001. Initially fresh stock LLaDA weights and optimizer; resumed from update 3,986 with saved optimizer and per-rank RNG state; Q/K/V adapters and a 16,793,601-parameter token-quality head (67,125,249 total trainable parameters). Microbatch 1 per GPU, accumulation 4, effective batch 16; 6,250 updates. Two training views per example; visible-token BCE plus 0.5 masked reconstruction CE. 40,000 clean and 60,000 corrupted examples; 12,000 each with 2–6 deranged interior city slots. Each 512-position response contains 87 answer/EOT tokens and 425 attended EOS reconstruction targets; quality BCE ignores the EOS tail; random response masks are independent in the two views. Final evaluation uses 512 positions, 128 diffusion steps, temperature 0, seed 42, one attempt, no repairs. After-stock uses the trained vocabulary with the head bypassed; after uses quality-guided remasking with the same 128-call budget. Before is the reused stock-model baseline on these exact 100 cases; its original provenance is preserved in baseline-reuse.json (local file). Baseline generation time belongs to the original run.

Complete tours count every city exactly once and close the cycle. Gap is computed only for complete tours, using coordinate distances. Invalid answers have no gap.
