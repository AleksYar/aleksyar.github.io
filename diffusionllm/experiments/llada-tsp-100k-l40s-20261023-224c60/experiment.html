100k-example TSP fine-tuning

Three independent adapters for the original LLaDA-8B-Instruct checkpoint. Data is generated and validated on the user’s Mac, then uploaded to Newton. Three independent L40S batch jobs start after the upload checks pass.

Training

One epoch, 6,250 updates, effective batch 16 (microbatch 2 × accumulation 8), BF16 frozen base model with FP32 LoRA parameters. Rank 16, alpha 32, dropout 0.05; all seven projection families inside transformer blocks, embeddings and vocabulary head frozen. AdamW, learning rate 0.0001, 5% warmup, cosine decay, weight decay 0.01, gradient clipping 1.0.

Response-only masked diffusion loss with inverse mask-probability weighting and no causal token shift. Prompts remain visible. Dynamic EOS padding, no truncation. Maximum sequence length: 1,024 for TSP5/10, 2,048 for TSP20. A memory fallback to microbatch 1 preserves effective batch 16 and is recorded if needed.

Data and comparison

Distinct integer coordinates sampled uniformly from [0,999]², randomly labelled city IDs. Instruction + coordinates + two nearest-neighbor hints, following the format used in our earlier experiments, inspired by LLMCoSolver. Exact target route plus distance to three decimals; calculations use unrounded Euclidean distances.

TSP5 and TSP10 use Held–Karp and reuse the previous 100 validation cases, excluded from training. TSP20 uses a subtour-elimination MILP with numerical optimality certificates and 100 new fixed validation cases. A timeout or uncertified label stops preparation; no approximate label is silently substituted. Generation is deduplicated by geometry and saved in resumable 1,000-row chunks.

Each dataset is generated on the Mac, tokenized with the pinned native chat template, checked for overlong records, and hashed. No GPU is held during data preparation.

Evaluation and saved evidence

100 answers before training, 20 fixed cases after updates 1,250 and 3,750, and 100 answers after the final epoch: 240 answers per experiment, 720 total. A separate four-update GPU profile saves one additional diagnostic answer, excluded from the main comparison.

All phases use 512 output positions, 128 diffusion steps, one block, temperature 0, one answer per instance, and the pinned official sampler. No route repair or repeated sampling. We report complete tours, exact optima, within-1% tours, gap on complete tours, strict output validity, objective consistency, response lengths and time. The final adapter is fixed at the end of epoch one; these are validation results, not a final unseen test set.

Every prompt, raw answer, full output token array, reference route, score, training update and final adapter is saved on the cluster. The two most recent complete optimizer/RNG checkpoints are retained for recovery. Reports and local dashboard snapshots are refreshed when results are collected.

Running without an internet connection

Data preparation moved to the Mac with user approval: Newton rejects CPU-only jobs, and the Darwin login currently lacks a compute allocation. Once data is uploaded and training jobs are submitted, the laptop can disconnect.

Submitted Slurm jobs run independently of your laptop. Public-partition jobs can be preempted; completed evaluation answers and training checkpoints are reused on requeue. Wall-time signals request a clean checkpoint and requeue. Local preparation resumes completed chunks after an interruption. Preparation failure prevents the corresponding GPU job from being submitted.

GPU request: account cs, partition public, one gpu:L40 resource on nlp-l40-1, with an in-job hardware check requiring NVIDIA L40S. The wall-time caps are safety limits, not duration predictions; a measured estimate is saved after the GPU profile.
