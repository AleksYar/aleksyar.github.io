WHAT REMASKING DID
- Repaired all six initially invalid tours.
- Shortened eleven initially valid tours.
- Changed seventeen distinct cases in total; made no valid incumbent worse.
- Increased optimal answers from 44 to 51, and within-1% answers from 72 to 80.
- Proposed 1,226 revisions and accepted 24 (1.96%). Some cases improved repeatedly.
- Produced 48 invalid intermediate proposals, all rejected by the acceptance rule.
- Used 99.58 model forward calls per case on average, including selector calls;
  the maximum was the authorized limit of 128. Initial construction used 16 calls.

Interpretation: the learned selection-and-refill process helped on this benchmark,
but proposal efficiency is low. The final 100/100 validity result belongs to the
whole inference pipeline, including retaining the best valid tour. It does not
mean every model proposal was valid, or guarantee validity on future instances.
This run did not compare learned selection with random or confidence selection.

OBJECTIVE AUDIT
Independently recomputed all initial and final scores and checked every revision
using original coordinates and unrounded Euclidean distances, including the closing
edge back to city 0. Final and initial stored objectives had zero discrepancy from
the independent calculation. Valid routes contain each of the 20 cities exactly
once before the closing zero. Rejected invalid routes have no scored objective.
Reference-route lengths, all acceptance decisions, unmasked-slot preservation,
case IDs, source hashes, checkpoint component metadata and forward-call accounting
also passed. Existing MILP optimality certificates were retained, not re-solved.
Tiny negative or positive optimality gaps at floating-point precision are numerical
roundoff, not solutions below the certified optimum.

The objective is calculated by code from the generated route. This evaluation does
not demonstrate that LLaDA learned to generate accurate numeric objective text.
Fixed city fields also supply the output structure externally.

TRAINING COMPLETED
Start: original 200k duplicate-loss checkpoint, excluding 10-epoch continuations.
Base: GSAI-ML/LLaDA-8B-Instruct. Existing rank-64 LoRA continued with a new prediction
head; no second LoRA was stacked. Frozen base weights remained unchanged.
Data: 500,000 fresh geometries, comprising 200,000 clean records and 300,000
correction/KEEP records. Exactly 500,000 distinct record indices were consumed once
in 31,250 optimizer updates. Effective batch size was 16.
Loss: masked reconstruction + 0.3 duplicate loss + 0.1 selector loss.
LoRA and head both changed; final trainable-parameter hashes matched on all four
GPU workers. The initial adapter matched the requested parent checkpoint exactly.
