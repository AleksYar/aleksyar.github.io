One versus two L40S GPUs

26 October 2026 · nlp-l40-1 · rank 64 / alpha 64 · standard masked diffusion loss

1.97× faster with two GPUs

Confirmed mean update time: 4.342 s → 2.200 s. Both configurations keep effective batch 16. Two cards reduce estimated training wall time by 49.3%.

Training-time projection

For 160,000 examples and one epoch: 12.06 hours on one GPU versus 6.11 hours on two GPUs. Estimated GPU-hours are 12.06 and 12.22, respectively. The estimate is ceil(examples / 16) × measured seconds per update. Queueing, loading, validation, saving, and preemption are additional.

What was tested

Frozen BF16 LLaDA-8B-Instruct, FP32 adapters in 224 projections, AdamW at 0.0001, dropout 0.05, weight decay 0.01, and the original response-only masked diffusion loss. The fixed 512-example training sample spans 959–991 tokens (mean 975.53), with dynamic EOS padding and no truncation.

Six execution settings were screened on both GPU counts, plus microbatch 16 with full activation checkpointing on one GPU. The fastest two safe settings per GPU count received independent longer repeats in reversed order. The second single-GPU repeat used the other allocated card. Selected configurations passed an additional test on the longest 16 sampled sequences with at least 3 GiB reserved-memory headroom.

DDP accumulates gradients locally and averages them once per effective update, before gradient clipping and the optimizer step. Each timed update includes forward, loss, backward, synchronization of gradients, clipping, optimizer, and scheduler. Timings are CUDA-synchronized and use the slower rank; model loading, metric collection, and result-file writes are excluded.

The deterministic CPU correctness gate matched the complete global-batch update within floating-point rounding. Real GPU trials checked finite losses and gradients, nonzero adapter changes, frozen base parameter samples, and identical full adapter hashes across the two replicas.

Short profiles do not establish convergence or final TSP quality. RNG consumption and padding can differ between layouts. These results apply to the tested model, loss, rank, sequence lengths, and PCIe GPU pair.
