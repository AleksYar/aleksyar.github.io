Reading the numbers. Valid = feasible tours; lower gap is better. Our gaps use recomputed tour lengths versus exact optima and average only valid tours: 1/50 untuned, 46/50 tuned greedy, 50/50 best of 8. The untuned mean therefore reflects just one tour. Paper values are reported as published, using its solver references and scoring.

Scope. All three of our rows use the same reused validation subset, not an untouched test set. Untuned means the Instruct checkpoint before TSP fine-tuning; its saved answers were rescored with the same evaluator. Best of 8 generated 400 candidates. The unified LLMCoSolver is a separate model trained across seven tasks; SFT means supervised fine-tuning and RL means reinforcement learning.
