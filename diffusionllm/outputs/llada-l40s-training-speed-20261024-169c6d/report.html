Scope and limits

  • Short profiles do not establish final model quality or sustained multi-day throughput.
  • Different microbatches change RNG consumption and dynamic EOS padding, so training trajectories need not be bit-identical.
  • No mixed TSP10–100 or long-context GPU benchmark was performed.
  • Learning rate was held at 1e-4 for both ranks; this measures execution settings, not hyperparameter quality.

Changing checkpointing should preserve the training objective; changing microbatch size can slightly change padding and stochastic draws. Finite gradients and nonzero adapter updates were checked. A short timing profile does not verify final solution quality.
