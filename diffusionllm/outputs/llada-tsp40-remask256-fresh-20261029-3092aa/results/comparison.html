IMPORTANT LIMITATION · BOTH TRAINED MASKING HEADS

TSP-only head and decoder: cannot answer general questions

This applies to the head trained in the 500k experiment and its additional 300k continuation. Both require city coordinates and route positions. The tested decoder supplies a fixed tour format and restricts predictions to city IDs. These heads do not provide general-purpose token remasking.

Underlying LLaDA + LoRA: the same weights can be run with ordinary text decoding, with the TSP head bypassed. That is a different generation procedure. These experiments did not evaluate general-question quality through ordinary decoding.

The reported TSP scores include this specialized decoder; they are not evidence of general-purpose chat with the head active.

Experiment contract

The TSP20-trained adapter and prediction head were reused without training. Size-dependent indexing, city choices and relative-position features were generalized to 10 and 40 cities. These are tests of transfer to new problem sizes.

Initial generation uses 9 useful calls for TSP10 and 16 for TSP40. The TSP40 initial block commits two or three cities per call to fill 39 internal slots; subsequent masks still cover at most 8 cities. The trained head then selects cities to mask, both digit tokens are replaced with the actual MASK token, and the model fills them again. Every completion becomes the next input. There is no rejection based on length, no retained best tour and no KEEP threshold. The 256-call cap includes selector calls; the last block can be shortened to fit.

Two sequential jobs used four independent L40S replicas on nlp-l40-2, with 25 cases per GPU. The original paired allocation estimate was 33–52 minutes, plus unknown queue and transfer time. Actual allocated time was 36.08 minutes and 2.406 GPU-hours. All 100 TSP10 references used Held–Karp; all 100 TSP40 references have a connected-tour MILP optimality certificate. References never entered model inference.
