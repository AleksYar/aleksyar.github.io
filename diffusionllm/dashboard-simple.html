Reading the results · TSP20 means a route through 20 cities. Complete tours visit every city once and return to the start. Optimal tours are shortest possible; lower gap is better. Gaps use valid tours only. Training scores are final validation results; datasets and scoring rules vary across experiments.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION · BOTH TRAINED MASKING HEADS

TSP-only head and decoder: cannot answer general questions

This applies to the head trained in the 500k experiment and its additional 300k continuation. Both require city coordinates and route positions. The tested decoder supplies a fixed tour format and restricts predictions to city IDs. These heads do not provide general-purpose token remasking.

Underlying LLaDA + LoRA: the same weights can be run with ordinary text decoding, with the TSP head bypassed. That is a different generation procedure. These experiments did not evaluate general-question quality through ordinary decoding.

The reported TSP scores include this specialized decoder; they are not evidence of general-purpose chat with the head active.
